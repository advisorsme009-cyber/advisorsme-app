# Requirements

## Financial Summary

### Drawer entry
Added a "Financial Summary" item to the sidebar drawer.

### Business Name header
Page header shows the page title plus a Business Name chip on the right.

### Income Statement tab
2x2 grid of charts: Revenue, Gross Margin, EBITDA, Net Profit.

### Balance Sheet tab
2x2 grid of charts: Working Capital, Fixed Assets, Shareholder's Equity, Debt Financing.

### Forecast year shading
Bars use a lighter blue for historical years and a darker blue for forecast years.

### Financial Highlights tab
Actual vs Forecast pivot table with rows for Revenue, Sales Growth %, Net Contribution, Margins, EBITDA, Net Income, Operating Cash Flow, Capex, Net Cash Flow, Equity Investment, Shareholder's Equity, plus a Terminal Value / ROI / ROE footer.

### Highlights export
Export button on the highlights table offers CSV download and Print / Save as PDF.

## Report Generator

### Business Name header
Page header shows the dashboard title plus a Business Name chip on the right.

### KPI cards
Three top cards: YTD Revenue (with YoY delta arrow), YTD Net Profit (with YoY delta arrow), and Cash Balance with a green "Healthy" dot.

### Performance Trend toolbar
The hero chart has a Monthly/YTD pill toggle, a Line Chart / Bar Chart pill toggle, % of Growth and % of Revenue checkboxes (mutually exclusive), and a metric selector.

### % of Revenue transform
When the % of Revenue checkbox is checked, every selected metric is rescaled to its share of that year's revenue and the axis switches to percent.

### % of Growth transform
When the % of Growth checkbox is checked, every selected metric is rescaled to year-over-year growth and the axis switches to percent.

### KPI Analysis paired bars
Horizontal bar chart shows % of Revenue and % of Growth side-by-side per year for the last 5 years.

### Commentary panel
Auto-generated insights are listed and the user can write a commentary in an input box at the bottom.

### Stacked sections
Four full-width stacked sections — Operational Expenses (blue), % of Revenue (purple), Annual Growth (teal), Common Size (grey).

### Chart / Table toggle
Each stacked section has a Chart / Table radio that switches between the stacked bar view and a tabular view of the same series.

### Export menu (chart mode)
When Chart is selected, the export button offers "Copy chart" (copies to clipboard) and "Download chart (PNG)".

### Export menu (table mode)
When Table is selected, the export button offers "Export as Excel" and "Export as CSV".

### White-background image exports
All chart PNGs (copy and download) are rendered onto a white background so they look correct when pasted into docs or dark surfaces.
